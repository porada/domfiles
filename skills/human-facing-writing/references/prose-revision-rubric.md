# Prose Revision Rubric

Treat this rubric as a selective diagnostic rather than a checklist. Evaluate only the dimensions relevant to the requested result, and do not require a finding for every dimension.

## Diagnostic Ratings

The ratings progress from a broken reading path to a choice that strengthens the work.

| Rating | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Breaks the reading path | The choice obscures purpose, weakens movement, creates avoidable friction, or damages trust. |
| Needs support | The intended function is visible but inconsistent, underdeveloped, or insufficiently earned. |
| Works | The choice performs its intended job clearly without drawing unnecessary attention to itself. |
| Strengthens the work | The choice materially improves understanding, momentum, experience, memorability, or trust. |

Use a rating only when it helps explain a material finding. Tie it to an observable point in the work, omit dimensions that already work, and never average ratings into a numerical prose score.

## Review Dimensions

### 1. Reader Contract and Stakes

Start by identifying the implied reader, what brings them to the work, the experience or change it promises, and how soon that promise becomes recognizable.

Watch for background before purpose, mystery without useful curiosity, interest the work assumes without earning, or an opening promise the body does not fulfill.

### 2. Controlling Movement and Necessary Nuance

Identify the central idea, question, event, experience, or inquiry. Then confirm that every major part develops that center rather than merely sharing its topic. Preserve any condition, contradiction, or ambiguity that materially changes the work.

Watch for drift, repeated restatement without development, incompatible claims, false certainty, or nuance restored too late to repair the premise.

### 3. Progression Within Each Reading Unit

Identify what changes from the beginning of each unit to its end. Determine the distinct job of every paragraph or internal section, then test whether removing or reordering it leaves a visible gap.

Watch for interchangeable parts, chronology without purpose, examples without inference, or units that reach their destination and continue without adding value.

### 4. Paragraph Function and Transitions

Identify each paragraph’s primary job and whether it has enough development to complete it. Read the logical relationship between adjacent paragraphs even when no transition phrase makes the relationship explicit.

Watch for mixed jobs, unsupported leaps hidden by short paragraphs, ornamental transitions, or fragments that imitate urgency without changing pace or meaning.

### 5. Concrete Material and Inference

Ask what each scene, example, detail, quotation, analogy, fact, or calculation contributes. Unless purposeful inference or delayed recognition belongs to the intended experience, confirm that the reader can understand why that material matters.

Watch for decorative anecdotes, stacked examples, isolated cases treated as typical evidence, unexplained numbers, or details detached from the work’s movement.

### 6. Clarity and Compression

Distinguish accidental opacity from intentional ambiguity, complexity, or delayed comprehension. When immediate clarity is intended, test whether each sentence yields its meaning on a first honest reading while preserving agency, causality, distinctions, and necessary qualification.

Watch for vague references, stacked abstraction, inflated transitions, cleverness that obscures meaning, or brevity that makes a complex thought falsely simple.

### 7. Rhythm, Pacing, and Emphasis

Observe how adjacent sentences gather, release, repeat, answer, or turn. Notice how the prose moves among claims, examples, scenes, calculations, dialogue, and inference. Judge sentence and paragraph length, fragments, lists, questions, and emphasis by their effect in context.

Watch for unrelenting staccato, unbroken density, sentence-length variation that leaves every sentence performing the same explanatory job, or paragraphs that repeatedly orient, explain, and recap as self-contained units.

Also watch for repeated syntax that neither develops the thought nor earns a purposeful break, lists used as default structure, repetition that performs no meaningful semantic, rhythmic, emotional, or structural work, or emphasis that replaces hierarchy and reasoning.

### 8. Voice and Reader Relationship

Identify the writer’s stance toward the reader, then judge whether it suits the subject, evidence, stakes, and intended experience. Keep confidence proportional to what the work has earned.

Watch for borrowed swagger, unsupported authority, false intimacy, generic encouragement, tonal inconsistency, manipulation, or a voice that eclipses the subject.

### 9. Openings, Boundaries, and Endings

Confirm that openings provide orientation and forward pressure without bait. At each unit boundary, look for a meaningful pause and the right amount of carried context. Confirm that endings complete the movement the body has earned.

Watch for generic scene-setting, delayed purpose, repetitive recaps, unsupported imperatives, arbitrary breaks, forced cliffhangers, or final flourishes detached from the actual stakes.

### 10. Cohesion Across Reading Units

Identify the distinct and necessary job of every unit in the complete work. Test whether their order builds cumulative understanding, stakes, evidence, experience, or action. Across the boundaries, track transitions, callbacks, recurring ideas or images, and deferred payoffs.

Watch for interchangeable units, repeated introductions or conclusions, lost context, recurrence without development, uniform units with no change of pace, or a collection that shares a topic but creates no cumulative movement.

## Review Result

Prioritize structural and meaning problems before line-level polish. Report the few findings that would most improve the work, distinguish required repairs from optional alternatives, and preserve intentional choices. For each required repair, identify the smallest revision that restores the reading path. If the work already succeeds at the requested level, say so directly and stop.
