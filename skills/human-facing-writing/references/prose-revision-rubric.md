# Prose Revision Rubric

Use this rubric selectively for substantive reviews, difficult revisions, and work composed from multiple reading units. Evaluate only the dimensions relevant to the requested result. Do not require one finding for every dimension.

## Diagnostic Ratings

| Rating | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Breaks the reading path | The choice obscures purpose, weakens movement, creates avoidable friction, or damages trust. |
| Needs support | The intended function is visible but inconsistent, underdeveloped, or insufficiently earned. |
| Works | The choice performs its intended job clearly without drawing unnecessary attention to itself. |
| Strengthens the work | The choice materially improves understanding, momentum, experience, memorability, or trust. |

Use a rating only when it helps explain a material finding. Tie it to an observable point in the work, omit dimensions that already work, and do not average ratings into a numerical prose score.

## Review Dimensions

### 1. Reader Contract and Stakes

Identify the implied reader, what brings them to the work, the experience or change the work promises, and how soon that promise becomes recognizable.

Watch for background before purpose, mystery without useful curiosity, assumed interest, or an opening promise the body does not fulfill.

### 2. Controlling Movement and Necessary Nuance

Identify the central idea, question, event, experience, or inquiry. Confirm that each major part develops that center rather than merely sharing its topic. Preserve conditions, contradictions, and ambiguity that materially change the work.

Watch for drift, repeated restatement without development, incompatible claims, false certainty, or nuance restored too late to repair the premise.

### 3. Progression Within Each Reading Unit

Identify what changes between the beginning and end of each unit. Determine the distinct job of every paragraph or internal section, then test whether removal or reordering creates a visible gap.

Watch for interchangeable parts, chronology without purpose, examples without inference, or units that reach their destination and continue without adding value.

### 4. Paragraph Function and Transitions

Identify each paragraph’s primary job and whether it has enough development to complete it. Inspect the logical relationship between adjacent paragraphs even when no transition phrase appears.

Watch for mixed jobs, unsupported leaps hidden by short paragraphs, ornamental transitions, or fragments that imitate urgency without changing pace or meaning.

### 5. Concrete Material and Inference

Determine what each scene, example, detail, quotation, analogy, fact, or calculation contributes. Confirm that the reader can understand its significance unless purposeful inference or delayed recognition is part of the intended experience.

Watch for decorative anecdotes, stacked examples, isolated cases treated as typical evidence, unexplained numbers, or details detached from the work’s movement.

### 6. Clarity and Compression

Distinguish accidental opacity from intentional ambiguity, complexity, or delayed comprehension. Where immediate clarity is intended, test whether each sentence can be understood on its first honest reading and whether the wording preserves agency, causality, distinctions, and necessary qualification.

Watch for vague references, stacked abstraction, inflated transitions, cleverness that obscures meaning, or brevity that makes a complex thought falsely simple.

### 7. Rhythm, Pacing, and Emphasis

Observe where the prose accelerates, pauses, enumerates, repeats, or intensifies. Judge sentence length, paragraph length, fragments, lists, questions, and emphasis by their effect in context.

Watch for unrelenting staccato, unbroken density, repeated syntax, lists used as default structure, repetition that performs no meaningful semantic, rhythmic, emotional, or structural work, or emphasis that replaces hierarchy and reasoning.

### 8. Voice and Reader Relationship

Identify the writer’s stance toward the reader and whether it suits the subject, evidence, stakes, and intended experience. Keep confidence proportional to what the work has earned.

Watch for borrowed swagger, unsupported authority, false intimacy, generic encouragement, tonal inconsistency, manipulation, or a voice that eclipses the subject.

### 9. Openings, Boundaries, and Endings

Confirm that openings create orientation and forward pressure without bait. Check whether unit boundaries offer a meaningful pause and carry the right amount of context. Confirm that endings complete the movement the body has earned.

Watch for generic scene-setting, delayed purpose, repetitive recaps, unsupported imperatives, arbitrary breaks, forced cliffhangers, or final flourishes detached from the actual stakes.

### 10. Cohesion Across Reading Units

Identify the distinct and necessary job of each unit in the complete work. Test whether the order creates cumulative understanding, stakes, evidence, experience, or action. Track transitions, callbacks, recurring ideas or images, and deferred payoffs across boundaries.

Watch for interchangeable units, repeated introductions or conclusions, lost context, recurrence without development, uniform units with no change of pace, or a collection that shares a topic but creates no cumulative movement.

## Review Result

Prioritize structural and meaning problems before line-level polish. Report the few findings that would most improve the work, distinguish required repairs from optional alternatives, preserve intentional choices, and identify the smallest revision that restores the reading path. If the work already succeeds at the requested level, say so directly and stop.
